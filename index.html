About us

More than an institution — a learning community

True education begins in the mind and flourishes through experience.

Dr. Soma Goho · Founder & Director

In a rapidly evolving world, Dr. Soma Goho stands as a visionary voice redefining education through innovation, neuroscience and purpose. An educator, author and entrepreneur, she is the Founder and Director of Promise Centre for Education and International Studies, Kolkata — a transformative initiative dedicated to nurturing the full potential of young minds.

Armed with a Ph.D. in Cognitive Brain Development and Entrepreneurship Management, her programmes focus on soft skills, brain development and emotional intelligence, blending scientific insight with creative learning to help children understand how to think — not just what to think.

  • World Leaders Summit, University of Oxford
  • London Book of World Records
  • PM Modi Inspiring India Award 2025
  • Emirates Award of Excellence, UNGPC Dubai
  • Author — “Little Leaders, Big Life Skills”
