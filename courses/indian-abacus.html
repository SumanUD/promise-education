About this programme

The first digital abacus tool — concentration you can measure.

Promise Centre is an authorised Indian Abacus centre, using the digital abacus rather than the traditional wooden frame. Children work through levels, first with the tool and then entirely in their heads, building the visualisation and concentration that abacus training is known for.

What your child will learn

  • Bead visualisation and finger technique
  • Addition and subtraction across multiple digits
  • Multiplication and division on the abacus
  • Mental arithmetic with no tool at all
  • Speed and accuracy under time pressure
  • Sustained concentration and listening

Quick facts

Age group5 years and above
ModeAt the centre, with online options
StructureLevel by level, each ending in an assessment
ToolIndian Abacus digital abacus
