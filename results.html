Published work

“Little Leaders, Big Life Skills”

Dr. Goho's book sets out the life-skills curriculum she has taught for two decades — the habits, language and confidence children need before academic content can do its job. It was published in June 2025 by Prashas Publication House (ISBN 978-93-49405-24-0).

She was also featured on the October 2025 cover of The Entrepreneur Bytes as a trailblazer in brain-based learning, and speaks regularly on cognitive science and women in education.
